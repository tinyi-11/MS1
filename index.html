Where do these thoughts point?

Point at a line to light up similar thoughts, then move along the lit lines to read them.

Each line is one thought. On the ring: about right now. Pointing in: looking back. Pointing out: looking ahead. A longer line means more classmates wrote the same thing. Color shows the topic.

There is no empty circle

Some thoughts leave. Some return. Others take their place.

A way of seeing it: recurring thoughts may not be problems that can be permanently completed or removed. Seeing them together makes repetition visible—not as a personal failure, but as part of a shared mental life.

The circle shows 139 thoughts from 32 classmates, collected once. It doesn’t show how thoughts change over time.
